### Formatting and commercial limitations

- Summary, Scope and Next Step preserve their saved plain-text content, not
  Markdown heading styles. Internal headings are ordinary lines.
- The PDF repeats the agency-entered scope under Services because line
  descriptions are already rendered there. Its scope continues onto page two;
  the projection above shows the actual break.
- Public Services combines the catalog description and entered work in one
  details cell. That cell collapses newlines; full Scope above retains them.
- The catalog's broad capability text still appears under Services. The actual
  Scope explicitly limits commitments to confirmed work; the manually supplied
  sample states its specific exclusions. No PDF/share template was changed.
- The agency must remove or resolve review notices and placeholders in its
  editable copy before using a real final proposal. A Ready label alone is not
  verification of approved deliverables. Historical snapshots remain unchanged.
- The automatic B draft is for scoping discussion, not a complete purchase offer.
  C lets a nontechnical owner see the proposed outputs and exclusions and discuss
  the illustrative investment. Feasibility, pricing and approval remain human
  decisions. Do not call C automatically generated or commercially approved.
